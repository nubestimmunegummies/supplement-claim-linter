    Why these phrases get flagged

    Each rule is based on a well-documented myth about skin brightening supplements:

    “Permanent” results
    Natural skin tone is largely set by genetics, through how much melanin the skin produces and the balance of eumelanin and pheomelanin. Some ingredients may help dark spots look softer over time, but they don't change a person's baseline shade.
    Results “in 7 days” or “overnight”
    Skin renews on a cycle of roughly a month in younger adults, and slower with age. Studies on oral glutathione typically measure results at 8 to 12 weeks.
    “FDA-approved”
    In the U.S., the FDA does not approve dietary supplements before they are sold.
    “Skip sunscreen”
    UV exposure keeps triggering new pigment. No supplement replaces daily sun protection.
    “Guaranteed results”
    Results vary with genetics, age, and daily routine.

    Background on these myths: Debunk Top Myths About Pills That Lighten Skin (NuBest editorial team).
